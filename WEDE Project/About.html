    Our Story

    Burkleigh House Bakery began as a small, family-run kitchen in Ferndale, Randburg, built on more than 25 years of commercial baking experience. What started as a family passion for honest, handmade baking has grown into a trusted supplier for over 70 retail stores, including some of South Africa's biggest grocery chains.

    Our Misson

    We believe in keeping things simple: the creamiest butter, the sweetest sugar, the fluffiest flour and the freest of free-range eggs. Every product that leaves our kitchen is made the way it always has been — by hand, with quality ingredients, and without shortcuts.

    Where we're Based

    Our bakery is located in Ferndale, Randburg, Johannusburg, where we bake fresh every weekday for our retail partners and local customers.
